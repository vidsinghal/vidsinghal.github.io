    Conference Papers

    LoCalMem: Type-Directed Adaptive Serialization for Location- and Content-Addressable Memory

    Published in ICFP 2026, 2026

    Formalizes Gibbon’s adaptive serialization, with mechanized soundness and locality proofs in Rocq and Lean.

    Recommended citation: Michael Rainey, Michael H. Borkowski, Michael Vollmer, Chaitanya S. Koparkar, Mikah Kainen, Vidush Singhal. (2026). "LoCalMem: Type-Directed Adaptive Serialization for Location- and Content-Addressable Memory." Proceedings of the ACM on Programming Languages, 10(ICFP), 461–493.

    Optimizing Layout of Recursive Datatypes with Marmoset

    Published in ECOOP 2024, 2024

    Compiler passes that optimize the memory layout of recursive algebraic datatypes, yielding speedups of 1.14x to 54x.

    Recommended citation: Vidush Singhal, Chaitanya Koparkar, Joseph Zullo, Artem Pelenitsyn, Michael Vollmer, Mike Rainey, Ryan Newton, Milind Kulkarni. (2024). "Optimizing Layout of Recursive Datatypes with Marmoset: Or, Algorithms + Data Layouts = Efficient Programs." ECOOP 2024, LIPIcs vol. 313, 38:1–38:28.

    Talks and presentations

    AoS to SoA Transformation of Packed ADTs

    December 13, 2025

    Poster, Midwest Programming Languages Summit (MWPLS) 2025, University of Minnesota, Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA

    Poster on array-of-structs to structure-of-arrays transformation for packed algebraic datatypes, which enables selective traversal and yields speedups of 1.1x to 12x.

    Optimizing Layout of Recursive Datatypes with Marmoset

    November 22, 2024

    Talk, Midwest Programming Languages Summit (MWPLS) 2024, University of Chicago, Chicago, Illinois, USA

    Talk on layout optimization for recursive algebraic datatypes, presented at the Midwest Programming Languages Summit.

    Optimizing Layout of Recursive Datatypes with Marmoset

    September 16, 2024

    Conference talk, ECOOP 2024, 38th European Conference on Object-Oriented Programming, Vienna, Austria

    Conference talk presenting Marmoset and its data layout optimizations for recursive algebraic datatypes, with measured speedups of 1.14x to 54x over the best prior work.

    Optimizing Layout of Recursive Datatypes with Marmoset

    April 26, 2024

    Seminar talk, PurPL Seminar, Purdue Programming Languages and Systems Research Group, West Lafayette, Indiana, USA

    Seminar talk on Marmoset, a compiler that optimizes the memory layout of recursive algebraic datatypes based on how programs traverse them.

    Cornucopia: A Framework for Feedback Guided Generation of Binaries

    October 10, 2022

    Conference talk, ASE 2022, 37th IEEE/ACM International Conference on Automated Software Engineering, Rochester, Michigan, USA

    Conference talk presenting Cornucopia, a fuzzing-based compiler framework for generating diverse binaries to evaluate binary analysis tools.

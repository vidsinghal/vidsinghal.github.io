    Privacy Policy

    This is a personal academic website. I do not run analytics, I do not set any cookies of my own, and I do not collect, store, or sell personal information about visitors. There is no comment system, no newsletter, no advertising, and no tracking pixels.

    What I do not collect

    I have no database and no server of my own. I cannot see who visits this site, and I receive no reports about individual visitors.

    Hosting

    This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub’s servers record technical information when a page is requested — typically the requesting IP address, user agent, referring page, and a timestamp. I do not control this logging, cannot switch it off, and have no access to the resulting logs. It is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

    Google Search Console

    This site is registered with Google Search Console, which is verified by a google-site-verification meta tag in the page source. Search Console reports aggregate statistics about how the site appears in Google Search — which queries surfaced it, and how many clicks and impressions it received. It does not identify individual visitors, and it sets no cookies on your browser. It is a tool for the site owner, not a tracker.

    Separately, Google and other search engines crawl and index this site, as they do any public website.

    Pages here link to external sites — publishers such as the ACM Digital Library and arXiv, conference websites, Google Scholar, GitHub, LinkedIn, and ORCID. Once you follow a link, that site’s own privacy policy governs. I am not responsible for their practices.
